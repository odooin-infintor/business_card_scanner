Business Card Scanner

Effortlessly digitize business cards into Odoo with our AI-powered scanning extension. Capture or upload a card, let AI extract the contact details automatically, review and edit before saving, then convert each scan into a Contact or a Lead — eliminating manual data entry entirely.

Features Highlight

Camera Capture / Image Upload

Capture a business card directly with your device camera or upload an existing image, on desktop or mobile.

AI-Powered OCR Extraction

Extract text from scanned cards using advanced AI models, including OpenAI GPT-4o and Google Gemini.

Auto-Fill Contact Fields

Extracted information is automatically mapped to name, phone, email, company, address, and website.

Preview & Edit Before Saving

Review extracted details and correct any field before the record is saved, ensuring accurate data.

Dedicated Scan Records

Every scan is saved to a dedicated Business Card Scan master record, separate from your main Contacts data.

Convert to Contact or Lead

Save scanned cards as a Contact in res.partner, or convert them directly into a Lead in the CRM pipeline.

Clean Scan History

Browse every scanned card in an organized All Scans list, with clear status of conversion to Contact or Lead.

Bring Your Own API Key

Connect your own OpenAI or Google Gemini API key for OCR processing, keeping full control of your AI provider.

1. Configuration

1.1) Activate the AI app from Apps.

1.2) Go to Settings → AI to choose your provider, Enable "Use your own ChatGPT account" or "Use your own Google Gemini account" and enter your API key.

2. Scanning Workflow

2.1) Capture or upload a business card image directly from the scanner screen.

2.2) Click "Extract Info" — AI extracts contact details automatically and presents them for review.

2.3) Preview and edit the extracted fields before saving the record.

2.4) View your full scanning history in the All Scans list.

2.5) Convert a scanned card into a Contact with one click.

2.6) Or convert a scanned card directly into a Lead in the CRM pipeline.

3. Mobile Experience

3.1) Scan, review, and save business cards directly from your phone — fully mobile-friendly.
